# GOSQA-27 – Authenticate Users and Provide Account Access

## Epic

**GOSQA-1 – User Authentication**

---

## Summary

Authenticate users and provide account access.

---

## Description

The platform should allow registered users to securely log in using their account credentials.

---

## Business Requirements

- Registered users can log in using a username or email address and password.
- The system rejects invalid login credentials.
- Successful authentication redirects users to their account or dashboard.
- User sessions are maintained during platform usage.
- Users can securely log out of their account.

---

## Related Test Cases

- TC-004 – Verify successful login with valid credentials
- TC-005 – Verify login fails with an incorrect password
- TC-006 – Verify login validation when required fields are empty
